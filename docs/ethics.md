# Ethical Considerations

This project considers several ethical issues related to the design and use of a university-based sharing platform.

## Data Privacy and Protection
UniSwap will handle personal data such as names, university email addresses, and contact preferences. To address privacy concerns:
- Only minimal personal data will be collected
- Data will be used solely for platform functionality
- Users will have control over their shared information
- Data handling will follow GDPR principles

## User Safety and Misuse
As UniSwap facilitates peer-to-peer exchanges, there is a risk of misuse or unsafe interactions.
- The platform will encourage on-campus exchanges
- No precise location tracking will be required
- Clear usage guidelines will be provided to users

## Fairness and Accessibility
The platform aims to be inclusive and fair:
- Free access for all students
- No prioritisation based on popularity or activity
- Accessible design principles will be followed

## Sustainability and Environmental Impact
UniSwap promotes ethical sustainability by:
- Encouraging reuse of books and study materials
- Reducing unnecessary waste
- Supporting environmentally responsible behaviour among students

## Academic Integrity
To prevent unethical academic behaviour:
- The platform will discourage sharing of restricted or assessment-related materials
- Clear rules will be provided on acceptable resource sharing
