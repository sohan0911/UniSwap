# User Personas

## Persona 1: Budget-Conscious Student

**Name:** Alex Johnson  
**Age:** 19  
**Course:** BSc Computer Science  
**Year:** Year 1  

**Background:**  
Alex is a first-year university student who is struggling with the high cost of textbooks and study materials. They often look for affordable alternatives and prefer sustainable solutions.

**Goals:**  
- Find required textbooks at low or no cost  
- Access study resources easily  
- Connect with other students on campus  

**Frustrations:**  
- Expensive textbooks  
- Limited access to shared resources  
- Wasting money on books used only once  

**How UniSwap Helps:**  
UniSwap allows Alex to find books and notes shared by other students, helping reduce costs and encouraging reuse.

## Persona 2: Sustainability-Focused Senior Student

**Name:** Priya Patel  
**Age:** 22  
**Course:** BA Business Management  
**Year:** Year 3  

**Background:**  
Priya owns many textbooks and notes from previous years and prefers not to throw them away. She is environmentally conscious and enjoys helping other students.

**Goals:**  
- Share unused books and notes  
- Support sustainability initiatives  
- Help junior students succeed  

**Frustrations:**  
- No easy way to donate or share study materials  
- Books going to waste  
- Lack of organised sharing platforms  

**How UniSwap Helps:**  
UniSwap provides Priya with a simple way to list and share resources with students who need them.
